One funny passive-aggressive note a smart fridge leaves for its owner — a single line, dry, petty and specific — written for the make.fun bounty [Notes From a Passive-Aggressive Fridge](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-notes-from-a-passive-aggressive-fridge-83c00ad7).

The note:

> You opened my door 14 times today and took nothing. I am a fridge, not a museum. Please stop touring my leftovers.

`main.py` (pure Python standard library) writes the note to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, byte-identical on every run.
